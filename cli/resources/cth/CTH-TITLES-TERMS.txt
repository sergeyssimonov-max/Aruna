CTH titles in this inventory
============================

The title shown beside each CTH number in the inventory is the title the
Catalog of Hittite Texts gives that number. The catalogue is

    Catalog of Hittite Texts (CTH) | S. Košak – G.G.W. Müller – S. Görke –
    Ch.W. Steitler, 2001–2026
    Hethitologie-Portal Mainz | G.G.W. Müller 2002–2026, Ch.W. Steitler
    2021–2026

    https://hethport.net/CTH/index.php?lang=EN

and the page states, quoted verbatim:

    This work is licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
    International License.

    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

What is reproduced
------------------

The titles only: one line of text per CTH number, and the mark "unassigned"
where the catalogue assigns no text to a number. Nothing else of the catalogue
is copied. The titles as reproduced here are made available under the same
licence, CC BY-SA 4.0.

What was changed
----------------

The text of each title is the catalogue's, word for word. What was changed is
its markup: the catalogue's italic and Sumerogram styling is dropped, the
superscript is kept, and runs of whitespace are reduced to one space. No title
is translated, shortened or reworded.

Where the snapshot comes from
-----------------------------

The titles were read from the page on the date and with the SHA-256 recorded
in the manifest of this package, section "cth_titles", and in the header of
cli/resources/cth/cth-titles.tsv in the Aruna repository.
